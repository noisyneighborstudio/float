Designed for Deep Focus

Every detail built for watching while you work.

Standard Picture-in-Picture breaks subtitles, hides site controls, and gets blocked by streaming apps. Float is a full WebKit browser stripped down to pure glass.

Automatic Video Fill on Any Site

Float finds the main playing video and stretches its player container—along with native captions, quality menus, and site controls—edge-to-edge. Recommendations, comments, and nav bars vanish automatically.

YouTube Netflix (DRM) Twitch Prime Video Apple TV+ Plex Vimeo Any HTML5 Video

17.8 MB. Zero Electron.

Written in ~665 lines of native Swift, Cocoa, and WKWebView. Launches in milliseconds, uses Safari's hardware video decode, and sips battery.

Smart Home Mosaic & One-Click Resume

Float learns the video sites you actually watch and builds a clean Home mosaic automatically. A glowing Resume card tracks your last video's title and exact timestamp—skipping short ads and trailers under 3 minutes.
